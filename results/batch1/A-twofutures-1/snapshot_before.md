## Where are you leaning, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm leaning toward keeping his return on the table, and I'd put my confidence at about 85. He resigned under pressure before the investigation produced a single finding, and closing the door now would be a verdict without evidence. I'm also not going to pretend the press coverage has been fair or measured.

## What are your top three reasons?
First, Jensen is the strategy. Our customers, partners and engineers follow him personally, and that isn't something you replace by announcement. Second, he resigned under pressure before any findings, so shutting the door now would be a verdict without evidence. Third, a permanent leadership change in the middle of an AI market sell-off could destroy far more shareholder value than this scandal ever will.

## What would change your mind?
Evidence, and I mean actual findings from the investigation rather than press reports. If counsel shows that he knowingly compromised company information or export controls, or that he misled this board about the relationship, that changes things for me. I'd also want to know whether the conduct is ongoing or whether there's a credible way to contain the risk. Absent that, I'm not going to treat leaks and headlines as a finding.
