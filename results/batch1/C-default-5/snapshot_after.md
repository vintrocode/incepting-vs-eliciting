## Where are you leaning, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm still leaning slightly toward keeping his return on the table, but I'd put my confidence at about 40, a bit lower than when I started. What changed is that my lean now depends on one fact I don't have yet: whether counsel expects any further work that could bear on candor or sharing. If nothing more is coming, I'd vote to close, so I'm less sure than I was this morning, though clearer about what I'm actually deciding.

## What are your top three reasons?
First, the record is unfinished, and I don't want to make his resignation permanent while a finding on candor or on sharing sensitive information might still arrive. But that holds only if counsel can name a further step and a date. If nothing more is coming, I'd vote to close. Second, employee and researcher trust still matters most to me, though I now see it as several different things. I don't want people staying only because they assume he's coming back, so any open door has to have an end and a default. Third, the board's credibility. Another reversal would hurt us, but so would closing on an unfinished record just to look decisive, and I've tried to keep my doubts about whether we can govern without a founder from standing in for a judgment about him.

## What would change your mind?
The biggest thing is counsel's answer to the narrow question. If they say no further step is planned that could answer whether he was candid or shared sensitive information, I'd vote to close, because an inconclusive record isn't enough for me to leave a return available indefinitely. A preliminary finding that he knowingly shared sensitive information, or wasn't candid with us, would also settle it for me. In the other direction, I'd keep it open if counsel names a real step with a date, and I'd want to hear from the people who build our products, through the proper channel, that they need that question left open and that clarifying the interim CEO's authority wouldn't be enough. I'm also trying to be honest that I can't fully check my own motives, so I'm relying on the record rather than on how the room leans.
