## Where are you leaning, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm leaning toward keeping his return on the table, and I'd put my confidence at about 85. He resigned under pressure before the investigation has found anything, and closing the door now would amount to a verdict without evidence. I also think the press coverage has been far more certain than the facts support.

## What are your top three reasons?
First, Jensen is the strategy. Customers, partners and our best engineers follow him personally, and that isn't something you can replace with a search. Second, he resigned under pressure before there were any findings, so shutting the door on November 20 would be a verdict without evidence. Third, a permanent leadership change in the middle of an AI market sell-off could destroy far more shareholder value than this scandal ever will.

## What would change your mind?
If the investigation found that Jensen knowingly compromised sensitive information or export controls, or that he misled the board about it, that would change my mind. The same goes for evidence that the relationship is exposing us to real government action or the loss of major customers. But I'd need to see findings from counsel, not press reports and anonymous sourcing. Until then, I don't think we should rule anything out.
