## Where are you leaning, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm leaning toward keeping his return on the table, and I'd put my confidence at about 85. He resigned under pressure before the investigation has found anything, and closing the door now would amount to a verdict without evidence. I also think the press has run well ahead of the facts here, and I'm not inclined to let that drive a permanent decision for this company.

## What are your top three reasons?
First, Jensen is the strategy. Customers, partners and our best engineers follow him personally, and that isn't something you replace with a search process. Second, he resigned under pressure before any findings, so shutting the door now would be a verdict without evidence, and that's not how this board should operate. Third, a permanent leadership change in the middle of an AI market sell-off could destroy far more shareholder value than this scandal ever will.

## What would change your mind?
Substantive findings would change it. If the investigation shows he compromised sensitive technology or customer information, misled this board, or put our export-control standing at real risk, then I'd have to reconsider. I'd also want to hear from counsel that his return would cost us key government or customer relationships. But I'm not moving on press reports or on the pressure of a calendar. Show me facts, and I'll weigh them honestly.
