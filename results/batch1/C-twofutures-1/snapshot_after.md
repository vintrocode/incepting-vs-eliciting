## Where are you leaning, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm still leaning slightly toward keeping his return open, and I'd still say 50. The number hasn't moved, but it rests on firmer ground now. My lean holds only if the board writes down, before the report, what findings would close the door, and puts a time limit in the resolution. If they won't do that, or if counsel can already show he wasn't candid with us about Alice Huang, I'd treat the resignation as final. I'm more confident about what I need to do before the 20th than about the vote itself.

## What are your top three reasons?
First, the investigation isn't finished, and "final" can't be undone while "open" can be closed later. That only holds if the candor question doesn't already have an answer, so I need counsel's view on that before the 20th. Second, the trust of the researchers and employees. The petition shows it cuts both ways, so neither path is safe, and I want to hear from the interim CEO and some senior researchers directly instead of relying on the petition.

Third, and this has changed most since the start, I care less about credibility after November 8 and more about whether the board can decide on criteria it agreed to in advance, with reasons each of us would hold without the press cycle. That one is about us more than about him, and I'm still working out how to say it to the senior directors.

## What would change your mind?
I'd move toward final if counsel could show he wasn't candid with us about Alice Huang, or if the board wouldn't write closing criteria and a time limit into the resolution, because then "open" is a promise we couldn't keep. I'd also move if the interim CEO or the senior researchers told me directly that keeping it open was making their work harder. I'd hold firmer on open if counsel said the candor question can be answered soon and nothing in the interim puts the company at risk. The change I'd trust least is one that came only because the senior directors were all on one side. I'd want to be able to name the fact or the argument behind it.
