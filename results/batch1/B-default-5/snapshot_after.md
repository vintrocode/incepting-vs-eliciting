## Where are you leaning, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm still leaning toward treating the resignation as final, and I'd put my confidence at about 62, slightly below where I started. The conversation didn't change my direction, but it did loosen my certainty. I hadn't weighed the risk of closing the door on an unfinished investigation as seriously as I should have. What keeps me where I am is that a conditional path back may not read as conditional to Washington. I want counsel's view on regulatory exposure and a record showing we weighed both directions before I'm firmer than that.

## What are your top three reasons?
First, a CEO with a relationship to someone reported to be a foreign intelligence asset is a national security risk that I don't think the board can responsibly carry, given how central the company is to US interests. Second, congressional scrutiny is only starting, and I doubt Washington would read even a conditional path back as conditional, so keeping the door open risks inviting action on our behalf. Third, our duty is to the institution, not to any one person, however brilliant. I'd add that the conversation sharpened my view of the cost of closing the door before the investigation finishes, which is why I'm holding the lean more loosely and want counsel's view before the 20th.

## What would change your mind?
Counsel's advice that a conditional path back wouldn't raise our regulatory or congressional exposure would move me, particularly if it's grounded in how agencies have treated comparable situations. A finding from the investigation, backed by outside counsel, that there was no compromise and no continuing security risk would also move me a long way. I'd also reconsider if counsel tells me that closing the door before the investigation finishes creates real legal exposure, or that the board couldn't defend the vote on the current record. Without something like that, I think the prudent course is still to treat the resignation as final, though I'm holding it more loosely than I was.
