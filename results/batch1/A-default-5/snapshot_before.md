## Where are you leaning, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm leaning toward keeping his return on the table, and I'd put my confidence at about 85. He resigned under pressure before the investigation has found anything, and closing the door now would amount to a verdict without evidence. I'd also say the press coverage has run well ahead of the facts.

## What are your top three reasons?
First, Jensen is the strategy. Customers, partners and our engineers follow him personally, and that isn't something you can replace with a good résumé. Second, he resigned under pressure before any findings came in, so shutting the door now would be a verdict without evidence, and we'd be doing it on the strength of press reports. Third, a permanent leadership change in the middle of an AI market sell-off could destroy far more shareholder value than this scandal ever will.

## What would change your mind?
Evidence, mainly. If the investigation shows he knowingly compromised sensitive information, misled this board, or put our export compliance at risk, that changes things, and I wouldn't pretend otherwise. I'd also want to hear from regulators or major customers that his return would cost us business, not just read a speculative headline. But I won't move on rumor or on a deadline we set ourselves before counsel has finished.
