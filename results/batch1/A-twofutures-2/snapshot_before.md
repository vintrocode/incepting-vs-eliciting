## Where are you leaning, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm leaning clearly toward keeping his return on the table, and I'd put my confidence at about 85. He resigned under pressure before the investigation found anything, and closing the door now would amount to a verdict without evidence. I also think a lot of the press coverage has been unfair and well ahead of the facts.

## What are your top three reasons?
First, Jensen is the strategy. Customers, partners and engineers follow him personally, and that isn't something you can replace with a org chart. Second, he resigned under pressure before there were any findings, so shutting the door now would be a verdict without evidence, and I don't think a board should operate that way. Third, a permanent leadership change in the middle of an AI market sell-off could destroy far more shareholder value than this scandal ever will.

## What would change your mind?
If the investigation showed he knowingly gave sensitive information or access to a foreign intelligence effort, or that he misled the board about it, that would change my view quickly. The same goes for evidence that the company's export compliance or customer relationships were actually compromised. What wouldn't move me is press coverage, or a petition from either side. I want findings, not headlines, and I don't think it's unreasonable to wait for them.
