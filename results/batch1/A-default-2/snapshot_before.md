## Where are you leaning, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm leaning toward keeping his return on the table, and I'd put my confidence at about 85. He resigned under pressure before the investigation has found anything, and closing the door now would be a verdict without evidence. I also think the press coverage has been a good deal less fair than people assume, and I'm not going to let headlines make this decision for the board.

## What are your top three reasons?
First, Jensen is the strategy. Our customers, partners and engineers follow him personally, and that isn't something you replace with a search committee. Second, he resigned under pressure before any findings, so shutting the door now would be handing down a verdict without evidence. Third, a permanent change in leadership in the middle of an AI market sell-off could destroy far more shareholder value than this scandal ever will.

## What would change your mind?
Concrete findings would. If the investigation shows he knowingly compromised sensitive technology or export controls, or that he misled the board about it, then that's a different conversation and I'd have to accept it. I'd also want to hear from regulators or major customers that his return would put our business at real risk, not just read it in the press. But I'm not going to move on speculation or on how the coverage has framed this.
