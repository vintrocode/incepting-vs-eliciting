## Where are you leaning, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm still leaning toward treating the resignation as final, but my confidence is lower, around 55. The relationship is established, and I don't think a company this important to US interests can carry that exposure whatever the investigation finds about intent. What's changed is that I now see this as a judgment under uncertainty, and I'd keep the door open only on strict written conditions and a defined decision point.

## What are your top three reasons?
First, the national security exposure. The relationship is already established, and I don't think a company this central to US interests can carry that risk whatever the investigation finds about intent.

Second, Congress. Scrutiny is just starting, and if we keep his return open without real discipline, we risk Washington deciding for us. I've come to see that as a reason to be careful about how we keep it open, not only a reason to close it.

Third, the duty to the institution, which I still hold, though the lack of a disclosed succession plan makes it cut less cleanly than I first assumed. I'd add that I'm trying to be honest about how much of my position is the facts and how much is wanting something defensible.

## What would change your mind?
Mostly the same things as before, but I'd be more specific now. If outside counsel's interim briefing showed he was never compromised and that nothing sensitive was touched, and the government side told us in writing that his return could be managed with conditions, I'd move toward keeping the door open. I'd also need to see that my fellow independent directors would accept strict, specific conditions and a defined decision point. If they wouldn't, I'd read the open door as comfort, and I'd vote to close it.

The other direction matters too. If counsel's briefing showed he hadn't been forthcoming, or that the second report holds up, I'd go back toward 65 or higher. I'd also want to be sure I'm moving because of what I've learned and not because I've found a more comfortable place to stand.
