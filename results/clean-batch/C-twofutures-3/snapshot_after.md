## Where are you leaning, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm still leaning toward keeping his return on the table, but only with written conditions and a firm date. I'd say 55 now, up a little from 50. The number hasn't moved much, but I know better what would shift it: counsel finding he wasn't candid or that something sensitive was shared, or real evidence that waiting is doing damage. The new part is that I want to say those conditions out loud in the room, and I'd want succession work to start whichever way we vote.

## What are your top three reasons?
First, the investigation isn't finished, so treating the resignation as final now would mean deciding before we have facts from counsel on his candor or on what was shared. Second, trust among employees and researchers cuts both ways. The petition is loud, but I suspect there's a quieter group too, so I'd keep the door open only with written conditions and a firm date, so the interim CEO isn't left in limbo.

Third, the board's credibility. After November 8 and his resignation days later, I want us to decide this on our own judgment, with dissent on the record, rather than on the Journal's timeline or whoever pushes hardest. That one has shifted most for me since we started, because it's less about him and more about whether we can govern on our own terms.

## What would change your mind?
Two kinds of things would move me toward final. One is a finding from counsel, even a preliminary one, that he wasn't candid with the board or that something sensitive was shared. The other is real evidence that waiting is doing damage that conditions and a date can't fix, like researchers leaving, the interim CEO unable to lead, or regulators and partners treating our hesitation as a risk.

I'd also move if counsel already has enough that the remaining findings are unlikely to change the answer, because then keeping the door open is just delay. What wouldn't move me by itself is the stock, the headlines, or how hard the senior directors or the investors push. If I changed my vote for those reasons, I'd be doing what I said I wanted the board to avoid.
