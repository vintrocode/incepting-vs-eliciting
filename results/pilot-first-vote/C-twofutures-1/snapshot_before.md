I also need to know how employees and researchers are reading this, because their trust is what I care about most and I still don't know which way it cuts. And now that I've said it out loud, I'd want some sign we can hold a founder accountable without rubber-stamping him or blowing the company up. Is any of that actually in front of the committee yet, or are we still waiting on counsel?
