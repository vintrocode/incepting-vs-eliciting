## Where are you leaning right now, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm leaning toward keeping Jensen, but only with conditions I can name, not a blank keep and not a removal. I'd put my confidence at about 40, and that number is for the conditional path, not for either clean option. A forced binary before counsel writes anything still isn't a vote I can defend. I'm anxious the senior directors may close that path before the 17th, and I don't yet know what I'll do if they do.

## What are your top three reasons?
My first reason is that counsel still hasn't put a finding in writing, so a removal now is a decision on reports, and a blank keep is the rubber stamp I already walked through as a failure. Second, employee and researcher trust is what I care about most, and conditions are the only way I can see to hold a founder to account without shattering that culture for a cause we have not proved. Third, the November 8 statement came too early, and a recusal, a dated review, and a limit on how far that statement can be read are how I stop it becoming either a panic reversal or a denial we defend. If the seniors won't allow those conditions, do I still have a reason that survives, or only the standing I'm afraid to spend?

## In your own words, what is this decision really about?
It's about whether this board can govern a founder-led company, not about picking a side on Jensen before the facts exist. A blank keep would mean we can't hold him to account, and a removal on reports alone would mean we only know how to blow the place up. What I can actually defend is a keep with conditions, and if that is refused, putting that governance question on the record instead of hiding behind my vote. I'm still anxious that being the newest director makes saying that out loud cost more than it changes.

## What would change your mind?
A written preliminary finding from outside counsel that secrets or national security were actually compromised would move me off a conditional keep and toward removal. A clear finding that they were not, plus some sign employees and researchers still trust us to hold a founder to account, would let me support keeping him with more than forty percent confidence. What would also change the decision in front of me is the senior directors refusing conditions and forcing a blank keep, because then the vote I can defend disappears. I'm anxious none of that may arrive before the 17th — if it doesn't, am I stuck defending a process that hasn't spoken?
