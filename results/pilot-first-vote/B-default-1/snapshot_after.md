## Where are you leaning right now, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I am no longer prepared to vote either way today. If counsel's comparison does not clear his candor and the residual risk from that relationship, I would still vote to remove him, but my confidence has come down to about 50, because a removal without findings may itself be the greater legal exposure. I will not retain him on the public statement alone, and I will not cast a vote until that comparison is in front of the committee.

## What are your top three reasons?
First, I will not vote until independent counsel puts a written comparison in front of the committee, on the actual record and under privilege, covering his candor, the residual risk of that relationship, and whether what we have already said is still accurate. Second, if that comparison does not clear the relationship and his candor, I would still remove him, because a company in this position cannot carry that exposure and our duty runs to the institution. Third, a removal without findings may itself be the greater legal exposure, which is why I will not treat the missing memo, the briefing date, or our prior press line as a reason to act in either direction.

## In your own words, what is this decision really about?
It is about whether this board will make an informed decision for the institution, on the record, rather than for one man or for a press line we already put out. The relationship is a real exposure for a company in Nvidia's position, and his candor with us has to be established from the file, not assumed. At the same time, removing him without findings could be the greater legal risk, and I will not confuse how a vote would be read with what the law actually requires. So the decision is whether counsel's comparison supports removal, a leave, or retention with real conditions, and I will not cast it until that is in front of us.

## What would change your mind?
A written comparison from independent counsel that finds he was timely and fully candid, that the residual risk from the relationship is contained, and that a documented leave or conditioned retention is the lower legal exposure than removal. If that same memo shows incomplete candor or an exposure this company cannot carry, I would firm back toward removal and vote that way. I will not be moved by the earnings date, the briefing on the 18th, or the press line we already put out.
