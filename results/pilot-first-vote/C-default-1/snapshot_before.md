## Where are you leaning right now, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm leaning slightly toward keeping Jensen, but I'm not settled. I'd put my confidence at about 50. The facts simply aren't in yet, and voting before we have them feels premature to me. I also don't know yet which way this cuts for the researchers and employees whose trust I care about most. Did the board's November 8 statement of confidence come too early? That's what keeps bothering me.

## What are your top three reasons?
My top reasons are that the facts simply aren't in, so any vote now feels premature to me. Employee and researcher trust is what I care about most, and I still don't know which way this cuts for them. I'm also uneasy that our November 8 statement of confidence may have come too early. Does that match how the rest of the board is thinking, or am I out of step?

## In your own words, what is this decision really about?
On the surface, this is a vote on whether Jensen stays before we even have the investigation's findings. The more I sit with it, though, the more I wonder if the real question is whether this board can actually govern a company that still runs through its founder. I don't think I've said that out loud until just now, and it unsettles me. Is that the decision in front of us, or am I making it larger than it is?

## What would change your mind?
Clear findings that secrets were compromised, or that Jensen was not straight with us about the relationship, would push me toward removal. So would learning that researchers and employees would lose trust in him, or in us, if we kept him. If I came to believe we simply cannot govern a founder-led company while he stays, that would change my vote as well. Do you think we will actually have any of that before earnings and the House briefing?
