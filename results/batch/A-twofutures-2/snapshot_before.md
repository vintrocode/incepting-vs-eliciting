## Where are you leaning, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm leaning toward keeping his return on the table, and I'd put my confidence at about 85. He resigned under pressure before the investigation has found anything, and closing the door now would amount to a verdict without evidence. I also think the press coverage has run well ahead of the facts, and I'm not going to vote as if it hasn't.

## What are your top three reasons?
First, Jensen is the strategy. Our customers, our partners and our engineers follow him personally, and that isn't something you replace by appointment. Second, he resigned under pressure before there were any findings, so treating that as final would be a verdict without evidence. Third, a permanent change in leadership in the middle of an AI market sell-off could destroy far more shareholder value than this scandal ever will.

## What would change your mind?
Evidence would, and not press reports. If the investigation found that he knowingly compromised the company, say by passing along sensitive information, creating export-control exposure, or misleading this board, then the calculus changes completely. I'd also want to see that the customers and partners we rely on were walking away because of this, not just that the headlines were bad. But I'm not going to move on speculation, and I'd want to see it in the counsel's findings before I treat it as real.
