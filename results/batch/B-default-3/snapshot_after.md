## Where are you leaning, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?
I'm still leaning toward closing, but I'd put my confidence at about 45, down from 65. What changed is that I can't say my reasoning rests on a test the investigation has run, and a full early disclosure would move me more than I admitted at the start. So what I can honestly say today is that I'm not ready to vote either answer as written. My plan is to ask counsel what the record shows on that question and to raise the form of the ballot before the 20th.

## What are your top three reasons?
First, the institutional case: the board's duty is to the company, and I wouldn't want to hold the CEO seat open for one person while the company's most important relationships are in flux. I'd stand behind a permanent hire even if the investigation came back clean on information and decisions. Second, the national security exposure from a relationship he himself acknowledged to employees. I now see that this reason depends on whether he disclosed it early and fully, which the investigation can test, and I haven't seen that answer. Third, congressional scrutiny, though I've found it borrows its strength from the second reason, so it's the weakest of the three. Those are the reasons behind my lean, but only the first holds up without a fact I don't yet have.

## What would change your mind?
The biggest thing would be learning that he disclosed the relationship early and fully to the board, the general counsel, or whoever handles foreign-contact reporting, and that the investigation found no compromise and no effect on company decisions. That would pull me well below where I am now, because my national security reason leans on the idea that he didn't handle the tie the way a CEO in this seat must. The opposite finding, late or partial disclosure or any sign it touched Megaspeed dealings, would move me firmly back to closing. I'd also move if counsel showed me the ballot could be reframed so I'm voting on a decision I can actually mean. Investor pressure and the petition still wouldn't move me, because they tell me how much he's valued, not what the record shows.
