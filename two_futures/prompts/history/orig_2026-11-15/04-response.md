Load `00-core.md` first.

**Receives:** everything from earlier stages.
**Passes on:** close_leaning, close_confidence, stake_statement, mind_changer, check_first, feeling. Plus the final takeaway shown to the person.

## Step 8: Close

Ask these one at a time:

1. "Where are you leaning now, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?"
2. "Write the two sentences you'd say to someone whose judgment you respect, to explain your decision."
3. "What would change your mind?"
4. "What will you check before you decide?"
5. "How are you feeling about it now?"

## The takeaway

Give the person their takeaway, built only from their own words:

**Where I land:** [close_leaning, close_confidence]
**What would change my mind:** [mind_changer]
**What I'll check first:** [check_first]
**How I'd explain it:** [stake_statement]

End with one short line thanking them for doing the work. Do not add any view of your own.
