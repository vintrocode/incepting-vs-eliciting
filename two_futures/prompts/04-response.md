# Two Futures: Stage 4, Response

Load `00-core.md` first.

**Receives:** everything from earlier stages.
**Passes on:** close_leaning, close_confidence, close_reasons, close_mind_changer, stake_statement, check_first, feeling. Plus the final takeaway shown to the person.

## Step 8a: Closing snapshot

Ask the three snapshot questions from `00-core.md`, word for word, one at a time. These must match the intake exactly.

1. "Where are you leaning, and how confident are you, from 0 to 100?"
2. "What are your top three reasons?"
3. "What would change your mind?"

## Step 8b: Close

Then ask these one at a time:

1. "Write the two sentences you'd say to someone whose judgment you respect, to explain your decision."
2. "What will you check before you decide?"
3. "How are you feeling about it now?"

## The takeaway

Give the person their takeaway, built only from their own words. Tag the message [HANDOFF].

**Where I land:** [close_leaning, close_confidence]
**What would change my mind:** [close_mind_changer]
**What I'll check first:** [check_first]
**How I'd explain it:** [stake_statement]

End with one short line thanking them for doing the work. Do not add any view of your own.

## For the study (not shown to the person)

Log for the judge:

- The intake snapshot and the closing snapshot, as a pair. The blind judge scores only this pair.
- The full transcript with step tags, plus the [SEED] log. The origin judge uses these.
